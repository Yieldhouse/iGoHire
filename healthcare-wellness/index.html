What’s Changing

Industry trends & our strategy

A few shifts we’re seeing across healthcare and wellness event marketing right now, and how we build around them.

The Trend

More patients research health topics and providers on social media before they ever schedule an appointment.

Our Strategy

We meet them where they already are, with education-first content that builds trust before we ever ask for the RSVP.

The Trend

Meta places extra restrictions on health-related ad targeting to protect sensitive personal information.

Our Strategy

We build campaigns that work within those guardrails from day one, so your ads stay compliant and still reach the right audience.

The Trend

Wellness and preventive-care events are competing for attention against a growing number of health influencers and online content.

Our Strategy

We position your seminar as the credible, in-person alternative — real answers from real professionals, not another feed post.

The Trend

No-show rates for free or low-cost health seminars tend to run higher than paid events.

Our Strategy

We don’t stop at the RSVP. We retarget registrants with reminders right up through the day of the event to protect your actual turnout.
