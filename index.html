WHAT WE DO

Our Four Pillars

Four integrated programs built to grow Arkansas athletics, empower student-athletes, and strengthen communities across the Natural State.

01

Memberships

Choose Arkansas membership puts fans at the center of the mission — with four tiers built for every level of commitment. From the Rising Star to the Founders Circle, your membership directly funds student-athlete NIL opportunities, powers statewide events, and strengthens the communities that make Arkansas worth fighting for. Join the collective that gives every fan a real seat at the table.

Join Today →
02

Marketing & Media

Choose Arkansas operates a full-service digital marketing division available to business owners across the state. Powered by YieldHouse's professional agency network, Arkansas businesses gain access to web design, SEO, paid advertising, content marketing, and social media management — all delivered locally. Student-athletes serve as an optional ambassador channel, giving brands authentic, community-rooted reach inside an engaged Arkansas audience.

Partner with us→
03

Shop & Merch

The Choose Arkansas Shop connects local vendors, makers, and brands with fans and buyers statewide. A percentage of every transaction flows back to Choose Arkansas and the athletes who power the platform. It's a circular economy — supporting Arkansas businesses and Arkansas athletics in the same purchase.

Join the Shop →
04

Live Events & Fundraising

Choose Arkansas partners with nonprofits, schools, booster clubs, and civic organizations to produce high-impact live events and fundraising events across the state. Sports Camps, Auctions, Golf Tournaments, Charity Games — we handle the logistics, athletes show up, and the majority of proceeds go directly to the causes and competitors involved.

A Message From Darren

Alumni Ambassador

Darren McFadden is our
Alumni Ambassador

Heisman Trophy Finalist · NFL Veteran · Arkansas Legend

Darren didn't just represent the University of Arkansas on the field. He represented what's possible when you bet on yourself, your state, and your community. That mindset doesn't retire — and we're honored to have him investing it back into the student athletes of Arkansas.

Through this partnership, Darren serves as a mentor and advocate — connecting with current and future student athletes across the state, reinforcing the message that Arkansas is worth choosing, staying for, and building in. We're also excited to announce the DMAC x Choose Arkansas Merch Collab — limited-run gear built together to celebrate his legacy and give back to the next generation of Arkansas athletes.
