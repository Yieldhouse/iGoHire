Membership Options

What makes Choose Arkansas unique is we give you, the fans, the option of where you want to donate your money. Multiple programs within a university...or multiple universities. The ball is in your court to where your money goes to work.

How It Works

Step 1. Locate which school/university you want to donate to.

Step 2: Select which program you want to donate to.

Step 3: Click the "Donate To" button and you will be redirected to the correct pledge
